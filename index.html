Features

Everything you need to manage your renovation

🤖

AI Project Planning

Generate complete renovation plans from photos and descriptions. Tasks, materials, costs, and timeline — all estimated by AI based on your location and skill level.

Task Management

Track tasks across phases with status, dependencies, and skill level indicators. Know what's done, what's in progress, and what's blocked at a glance.
